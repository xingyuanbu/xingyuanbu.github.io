ABOUT ME

Researching multimodal
intelligence.

I build models and agents that understand, reason about, and generate visual content.

I am a Staff Research Engineer and Tech Leader at ByteDance / TikTok, working on multimodal understanding and generation. My current research spans video reasoning and grounding, multimodal agents, and creative video generation, from large-scale model training to real-world applications.

Previously, I was a Senior Algorithm Engineer and Tech Leader at Alibaba, developing large language models and multimodal representations for e-commerce search ads. Before that, I worked on computer vision and neural architecture search at SenseTime, and object detection at Baidu. I received my M.S. from Beijing Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Yuwei Wu and Prof. Yunde Jia.

Research interests

  • Multimodal understanding & generation
  • Large language models & agents
